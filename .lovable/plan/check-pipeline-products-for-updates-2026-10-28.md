## Steps

1. For each product, check public sources only: the vendor website and news pages, the FDA 510(k)/De Novo database, EUDAMED and notified-body notices, the NMPA/TGA/other recognised registers, and ASTRO 2026 / ESTRO / AAPM 2026 announcements.
2. Sort each product into one of these:
   - **Now cleared/certified** (meets the catalogue inclusion gate): move it from pipeline into its proper category, with the clearance number, date and source. Old URLs keep working.
   - **Still pipeline, new info** (new name, stage, publication, expected date): update the fields, with sources and retrieval dates.
   - **No change**: only refresh the "last checked" date.
   - **Withdrawn / no longer mentioned**: flag it for you; nothing is removed without a source.
3. Look for new pre-market RT AI products announced at ASTRO 2026 and list them as candidates. They are not added without your OK.
4. Give a short report: what changed, the source for each change, and what couldn't be confirmed.

## Rules
- Only facts that can be checked; every changed field gets a disclosed source (sourceAccess + sourceRetrievedOn for non-public sources).
- No change to the site's design or pipeline page behaviour.
- Run the existing product tests and the evidence checker after the edits.
