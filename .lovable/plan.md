# Add EN 18286:2026 (first harmonised standard under the AI Act)

Source: European Commission, "First harmonised standard supporting the AI Act published in the Official Journal", 19 Oct 2026.

## Where it goes
Standards & guidelines → "Quality & Software Lifecycle" card, next to ISO 13485:
- **EN 18286:2026** — Quality management system for EU AI Act regulatory purposes (CEN/CENELEC).
- Scope: harmonised standard giving presumption of conformity with AI Act Art. 17 (QMS) for high-risk AI providers; designed to layer on ISO 13485 / ISO/IEC 42001 rather than replace them.
- Version: "EN 18286:2026 — referenced by Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2274".
- Status: standard; lastVerified 2026-10-20.

Also a one-line mention in the ISO/IEC 42001 entry's note that EN 18286 (not 42001) carries presumption of conformity for Art. 17.
