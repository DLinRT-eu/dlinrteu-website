# One-by-one check of the 186 new papers

## Confirmed

- Deadline for reviewers: 11 December 2026 (11-12).

## What each paper gets
For every paper (title, abstract, authors and affiliations from Marlie's file; DOI/PubMed lookup when the abstract is missing or unclear):
1. **Product check.** Does the paper actually use the named commercial product, and the AI/deep-learning part of it? For example, a Synchrony paper using only classic tracking, or a RayStation paper using atlas contouring, doesn't count. The right product(s) get confirmed or corrected.
2. **Include decision.** One of:
   - Include: peer-reviewed, product clearly used.
   - Include with note: e.g. product is one arm of a comparison, or a version caveat.
   - Exclude, with a reason: not the AI function, product not named, review/editorial without new data, conference abstract, duplicate, or not radiotherapy.
3. **Suggested score.** Evidence Rigor E0–E3 and Level of Efficacy L1t–L6, plus the study flags (vendor-independent, multi-center, multi-national, prospective, external validation), with a one-line rationale. This replaces the keyword-based first score.
4. **Action.** Add to which product page, or drop, or ask Marlie.

## How
- The 186 papers are split into about 6 batches by product group (Synchrony; Varian Ethos/Eclipse; RayStation; MVision; Philips MRCAT/Spectronic; the rest), and checked in parallel.
- Each batch follows `docs/review/GUIDE.md` and the score definitions on the site. Nothing is guessed: when the abstract doesn't settle the product use or study design, the paper is marked "uncertain — reviewer to check full text".
- I spot-check a sample from each batch for consistency.

It adds an "Excluded" summary sheet and updated reviewer workload counting only included papers.
