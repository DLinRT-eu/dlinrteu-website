# Add screened papers to product pages

## Goal
Add the 57 "Include" / "Include with note" papers to their product pages, and resolve the 29 "Uncertain" papers using full text and other sources, adding those that qualify.

## Steps
1. **Resolve the 29 uncertain papers**
   - Look up each paper in OpenAlex, PubMed/Europe PMC (open full text) and Crossref: methods sections, funding/conflict statements, affiliations.
   - Decide again: include, include with note, or exclude. Anonymised products (e.g. "AIS-1 to AIS-4") are only added when the paper or its supplement names the product.
   - Record the source used for each decision.
2. **Check every paper before adding**
   - Confirm DOI, title, authors, journal and year against Crossref/PubMed (no guessed details).
   - Skip any already on the page (duplicate check by DOI/PMID/title).
   - Confirm the AI function of the product was used; papers that use only a non-AI function are left out.
3. **Add to product pages**
   - Add each paper in the site's usual evidence format, with the suggested Evidence Rigor (E0–E3) and Levels of Efficacy (1t–6), study quality flags, and a short note for "with note" cases (e.g. product is one arm of a comparison).
   - Re-check each product's overall evidence level; change it only if the new papers support it.
   - Stamp the products as revised today.
3. **Reviewer follow-up**
   - Scores stay provisional for the assigned reviewers (deadline 2026-12-11); the assignments sheet is updated with "added to site" status.
4. **Checks**
   - Run tests and the evidence checker; open a sample of changed product pages at desktop and phone width.
5. **Deliverables**
   - `evidence-screening-assignments-2026-11-19_v3.xlsx` with final decisions for the uncertain papers and the added/skipped status.
   - Short summary report: papers added per product, uncertain papers resolved and how, anything still unresolved.

## Not included
- Excluded papers (100) stay off the site.
- No new review tasks are created in the app.
